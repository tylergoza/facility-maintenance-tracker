What it can read

JSON from {{.APIBase}}:

  • GET /products: what there is and how many in all. Filter with q, category, place, tag, portable=1, counted=1; a place or tag totals only what's there.
  • GET /products/{id}: a product and every item of it, with where each is.
  • GET /items: items, by q, place (add direct=1 for only that place), tag, product, portable=1.
  • GET /items/{id}: an item, with its numbered units and their IDs unless it's only counted.
  • GET /supplies, GET /supplies/{id}: supplies on hand, in use and out for cleaning; filter like items, plus low=1.
  • GET /places, GET /places/{id}: the tree of places, and everything in one.
